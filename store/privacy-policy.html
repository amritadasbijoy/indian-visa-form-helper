Privacy Policy

Indian Visa Form Helper – Bangladesh (Unofficial) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Chrome extension helps people in Bangladesh fill the Indian visa application at indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in. It is an unofficial tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the Government of India, the High Commission of India or IVAC.

What data the extension handles

You type or scan the details the visa form asks for. These can include:

  • names, date and place of birth, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses;
  • passport details, national ID number, family and marital details;
  • the applicant's photo, passport scans and supporting documents (PDF);
  • trip details: dates, ports, hotels, hospitals and references;
  • Temporary IDs, Application IDs and the status of submitted applications.

Where the data is kept

All data is stored only in your own browser, in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. The developer does not keep a copy of your applicants: the helper server (below) only reads what you send it for a scan or a search and stores none of it. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.

Removing the extension deletes this data. You can also delete applicants and records inside the extension at any time. Backups are files you save yourself; they stay wherever you keep them.

When data leaves your computer

Data is sent only in these cases, and only when you start the action:

  • The visa website. When you fill a page, the extension types your saved details into the official form at indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in. You submit the form yourself: the captcha and the final submit are always done by you.
  • Optional AI features. "Scan with AI" and "Find hotels" work only after you add your own API key for an AI service you choose (Anthropic, or an OpenAI-compatible service such as River AI, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or a local Ollama). When you use them, the image you scan, or the city you search for hotels, is sent directly from your browser to that service. That service's own privacy policy applies. If you do not add a key, nothing is sent to any AI service. The normal "Scan passport" button uses the server scan or "This computer only", as you choose (below).
  • Server passport scan (optional). If you choose "Server scan" for passports, the passport image is sent to the Visa Helper server at ivh.techistbd.com, where a text reader (OCR) on that server reads it and returns the text. No AI service or other company is involved. The image is read in memory and not stored, and there is no log of it; only a daily count per connection is kept (from a one-way code of the address, not the address). "This computer only" scans never leave your computer.
  • Helper AI (invited users only). If someone gave you a personal access code and you choose "Helper AI" in Settings, the image you scan (or the city you search for hotels) is sent to the helper server at ivh.techistbd.com, which passes it to River AI and returns the answer. The server does not store images or answers; it keeps only a count of requests per access code and month, to apply the monthly limit.
  • Agency sync (optional, invited users only). If you use "Agency sync", your applicant list (without documents and PDFs) is encrypted on your computer with your agency password and then stored on ivh.techistbd.com so your other computers can download it. The server cannot read it: the password never leaves your computers.
  • Hotel search. "Find hotels" sends only the city you type to ivh.techistbd.com. The server looks up hotels in OpenStreetMap, on the hotels' own websites and with Google Places, using only that city name. No applicant data is sent; only a daily count per connection is kept.
  • Directory. The Hotels and Hospitals "Directory" button downloads a public list of checked places from ivh.techistbd.com. Nothing is sent.
  • Form updates. About once an hour while you use the visa site, the extension downloads a small public list of the visa form's field names from ivh.techistbd.com, so it keeps working when the site renames a field. No personal data is sent with this request, and the server does not keep a log of it.

Your API key and Helper AI access code are stored only in your browser. They are never included in backups. Backups can be locked with a password; the "Lock with a PIN" setting hides the panel on shared computers.

The applicant details form at ivh.techistbd.com/family works only on your phone: it makes a file for you to send, and uploads nothing.

What the developer does not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, credit checks or any purpose other than filling your visa application.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Storage: to keep your applicants, hotels and settings on your computer.
  • Side panel: to show the helper next to the visa website.
  • Access to indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in: to fill the visa form pages.
  • Access to an AI service (optional): asked for only when you set up that service.

Children

Parents or guardians may enter a child's details to apply for that child's visa. The data is handled exactly as described above.
